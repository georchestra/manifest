# Project Steering Committee (PSC)

geOrchestra is represented by a Project Steering Committee (PSC) composed of individuals actively involved in the project.

PSC members do not represent any company, organization, or institution. They act in the best interest of the project and its community.

The PSC ensures that the founding principles of geOrchestra are respected, as defined in [the geOrchestra manifest](https://github.com/georchestra/manifest/blob/main/MANIFEST.EN.md): free, modular and interoperable software. To this end, it:

* defines and maintains the project's contribution rules;
* ensures that everyone's voice is respected and that the entire community participates;
* promotes an open, transparent decision-making process based on consensus-building;
* ensures the consistency of the project roadmap;
* reviews and votes on geOrchestra Improvement Proposals (GIPs).

## Operations

The PSC meets at least once a month.

Consensus is sought for all decision-making. When this cannot be reached, the PSC may call upon the geOrchestra community to obtain a decision.

PSC decisions, including those related to GIPs, are publicly documented, in accordance with [the voting process](https://github.com/georchestra/improvement-proposals/blob/main/README_en.md#process).

## Nomination of New Members

PSC members are co-opted based on:

* their contributions to the project;
* their engagement within the community;
* their ability to act in the best interest of geOrchestra.

Any proposal for the appointment of a new member is reviewed by the PSC, which then approves or rejects the admission of the proposed member to the PSC.

## End of Membership and Participation

Any member may leave the PSC at any time by notifying the PSC via email.

PSC membership is attached to an individual and may not be transferred or delegated. Anyone wishing to join the PSC must follow the nomination process described in this document.

PSC members are expected to actively participate in the work of the committee and in the life of the project.

When a member no longer participates in the activities of the PSC or the project for an extended period, the PSC may initiate a discussion with the individual in question to determine whether they wish to retain their role. Following this discussion, the active members of the PSC may decide to terminate that person's mandate.
